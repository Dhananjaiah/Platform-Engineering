## The five Staff muscles
1. **Influence without authority**: win teams with a better path, evidence, and help.
2. **Adoption and standardisation**: migrations, deprecations, RFCs/ADRs.
3. **Strategy**: multi-quarter roadmap, tied to business outcomes, with explicit trade-offs.
4. **Stakeholders**: app teams, security, SRE, architecture, product, leadership, across time zones.
5. **Technical leadership**: design reviews, direction, mentoring, raising the bar of others.

## Say it in 2 minutes
"I lead by making the right thing the easy thing: build the better path, prove it with data, help teams migrate, and write down decisions as RFCs/ADRs so they outlast me. I set a roadmap from developer pain and business outcomes, review designs for the whole platform team, and grow engineers. With executives I speak in time saved, risk reduced and money; with engineers in what gets easier on Monday."
